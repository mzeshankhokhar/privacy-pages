🏁 Drift Racing

Mountain, Speedway & Drift Circuits

An offline arcade racer with three real circuits and eight layouts. No account, no sign-up, and nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

Drift Racing is a single-player racing game for Android: a 2.8 km mountain circuit, a banked speedway with an infield road course running through tunnels beneath it, and a drift circuit — each raceable in either direction. Steer by tilting the phone, by a round steering wheel, or with arrow buttons. The game runs entirely offline; the only thing that uses the network is the advertising described in the privacy policy.
