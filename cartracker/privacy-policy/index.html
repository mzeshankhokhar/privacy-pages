Short version: Car Tracker records your car's location so that you can see it on your phone. That data goes from the car straight into a folder in your own Google Drive and is read from there by your phone. We run no server, we have no accounts, and we never receive, see or sell your location or any other data. There are no ads and no analytics in the app.

1. Who we are

Car Tracker is an Android app published by an independent developer. Contact: mzeshankhokhar@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

The same app runs in one of two modes, chosen when it is first opened:

  • Tracker mode — on the car's Android head unit. While the head unit is on (which normally means the ignition is on), it records the car's position and uploads it to your Google Drive.
  • Viewer mode — on your phone. It reads the same Google Drive folder to show the car's current position and its trip history.

3. Data the app records

In tracker mode, the app records:

  • Precise location of the head unit (latitude and longitude from its GPS), about every 5–10 seconds while driving and every 30 seconds while standing with the ignition on, together with GPS time, speed, direction of travel, altitude, accuracy and the number of satellites
  • Events: when the car stops and moves off, when the ignition goes on and off, and when the GPS signal is lost or comes back
  • The vehicle name you type in the app, a random vehicle ID created by the app, and the app version

The app does not record anything when the head unit is switched off. It does not access contacts, messages, photos, microphone or camera.

4. Where the data is stored

  • On the head unit: every record is first saved in the app's private storage, so nothing is lost while there is no internet. Records already uploaded are deleted from the head unit after 30 days.
  • In your Google Drive: the app uploads the records into a folder named CarTracker in the Google account you sign in with — a file with the car's latest position (live.json) and compressed history files, one folder per day.

The data travels directly between your devices and Google Drive over encrypted HTTPS. It never passes through a server of ours, and we have no access to your Drive.

5. Google account access

To use Google Drive, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant only these permissions:

  • Google Drive — only files created by this app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). The app can create and read the files it made itself; it cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address (openid, email), only to show on screen which account is signed in.

The sign-in token that lets the app upload is stored on the device, encrypted with the Android Keystore, and is used only to talk to Google Drive.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Car Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the tracking and viewing features described here; it is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

7. Viewing on your phone

  • The viewer downloads the car's files from your Google Drive and keeps copies of the history files in the app's private cache, so they open faster next time.
  • Street address: to show the address of the car's position, the viewer uses Android's built-in Geocoder, which sends the coordinates to your phone's location service provider (normally Google).
  • Map: map images come from OpenFreeMap. To draw the map, your phone requests the map tiles for the area on screen, so that service sees your IP address and which map area is being viewed — not who you are or which car it is.
  • Directions in Google Maps: only if you tap this button, the car's position is passed to the Google Maps app on your phone.

8. Permissions the app requests

  • Precise location, including "allow all the time" (tracker mode) — to record the car's position while another app is on the head unit's screen.
  • Foreground service and notifications — to keep recording, with a visible "Tracking" notice.
  • Start at boot and ignore battery optimisations — so recording starts by itself when the head unit powers on and is not stopped by the system.
  • Internet and network state — to upload to and read from Google Drive, and to know when the internet is back.
  • Wake lock — to finish saving and uploading for a few seconds after the ignition is turned off.

9. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive — storage of your data in your own account. Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data) — map images in the viewer. openfreemap.org.
  • Android Geocoder — street addresses in the viewer, provided by your phone's location service.

There is no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

10. Data retention

Files in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them. On the head unit, uploaded records are deleted after 30 days; records not yet uploaded are kept until they can be uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on that device.

11. Data security

Data on the devices is kept in Android's private, per-app storage. Uploads and downloads use encrypted HTTPS connections to Google. The sign-in token is encrypted with the Android Keystore. Access to the Drive files is protected by your Google account.

12. Your rights & choices

  • Stop recording: use Pause tracking in the app on the head unit, or uninstall it.
  • Stop uploading: tap Sign out in the app. This also cancels the app's access token.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the data: delete the CarTracker folder in Google Drive, and uninstall the app (or clear its storage) on the head unit and the phone.

13. Other drivers

Car Tracker records where the car goes, whoever is driving. If other people drive a car with the app installed, please tell them that the car's location is recorded. Use the app only for vehicles you own or are allowed to track.

14. California privacy rights

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We do not receive your data at all; it stays in your own Google account and on your own devices, under your control.

15. Children's privacy

Car Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
