🚗 Car Tracker

Live Location & Trip History

Your car's Android head unit records where the car goes, and your phone shows it live — stored only in your own Google Drive. No company server, no account with us.

Car Tracker is one Android app with two modes. Installed on the car's Android head unit, it records the car's GPS position every few seconds while the ignition is on, notices when the car stops and when the ignition goes off, and keeps everything on the unit until it has been uploaded — so nothing is lost when there is no signal. It saves the data to a CarTracker folder in your own Google Drive.

Installed on your phone in viewer mode, signed in with the same Google account, it reads that folder to show where the car is right now — driving, stopped or parked — and every drive of the day on a map, with distance, time, stops and a replay.
